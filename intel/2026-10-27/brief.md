## Top summary

Agentic banking is moving from concept to controlled infrastructure: Socure launched tools to detect, assess, and bind AI agents to verified principals, while Q2 positioned agentic interfaces as a new banking channel requiring layered fraud controls.[REF 1][REF 2] Sezzle’s national-bank application extends the fintech-to-bank trend and could expand its role across deposits and credit products.[REF 3] Alkami is reinforcing identity-centric, layered account-opening defenses through its MANTL–Alloy integration as generative-AI fraud scales.[REF 4]

## Material signals

### Socure launches Agent Intelligence for AI-agent identity and trust

Socure launched Agent Intelligence to detect AI agents, assess their trustworthiness, and connect each agent to the verified person or business that authorized it.[REF 1] The offering includes Agent Detection, generally available within Socure’s Digital Intelligence product; Agent TrustScore, available to beta customers; and Agent Credential, planned for general availability in 2027.[REF 1]

**ASA relevance:** This materially advances partner capabilities around agent identity, delegated authority, and action-level risk assessment, informing ASA Auth and Verify planning for agent-mediated financial activity.

### Q2 positions agentic banking as a new channel with integrated fraud controls

Q2 described personal AI agents as an emerging banking channel that will extend financial institutions’ brands, security, and controls into agentic interactions.[REF 2] Its positioning includes identity checks at login, high-risk-event monitoring, transaction monitoring, session monitoring, early Q2 Assistant adoption, and account-takeover defenses.[REF 2]

**ASA relevance:** Q2’s framing raises the competitive bar for authentication, verification, session risk, and platform controls in financial-institution distribution. It also reinforces the need to treat agentic access as a channel-control problem rather than solely a conversational interface.

### Sezzle applies for an OCC national bank charter

Sezzle applied to the OCC to establish Sezzle Bank, N.A., and separately applied to the FDIC for deposit insurance.[REF 3] The proposed bank would support expansion beyond buy now, pay later into deposits, checking, savings, and credit products; the formal review process is expected to take 12 to 18 months.[REF 3]

**ASA relevance:** If approved, Sezzle’s broader digital-banking footprint could increase demand for transaction intelligence and predictive cash-flow capabilities among digitally native consumers.

### Alkami highlights MANTL–Alloy controls against generative-AI account-opening fraud

Alkami highlighted the integration of MANTL Onboarding & Account Opening with Alloy’s AI-powered identity and fraud-prevention platform.[REF 4] The combined approach uses layered, identity-centric controls to identify suspicious activity earlier, automate identity verification, and improve account-opening decisions as AI-enabled fraud becomes faster and more scalable.[REF 4]

**ASA relevance:** The integration strengthens a channel partner’s account-opening fraud stack and informs ASA Auth and Verify positioning, particularly around layered identity, device, and fraud controls.
