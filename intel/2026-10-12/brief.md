### FIS launches agentic CFO intelligence suite

FIS launched the Agentic Insights Suite for CFO teams, combining cash-flow forecasting, collections, liquidity, risk management, recommendations, and unified financial data.[REF 3][REF 4] The suite includes multi-horizon forecasting and scenario simulations for delayed inflows or faster outflows, creating direct competitive overlap with ASA Forecast and Compass.[REF 3][REF 4] Its unified data and reporting layer also overlaps with ASA One View.[REF 3]

### Corelation embeds Unit21 fraud and AML workflows in KeyStone

Corelation and Unit21 integrated AI-native fraud prevention and AML monitoring into the KeyStone credit-union core platform.[REF 2] The integration supports bidirectional data and workflow movement: member, account, card, and transaction data flow into Unit21 for detection and investigation, while analysts can act through KeyBridge to block cards, stop payments, and place holds.[REF 2] This strengthens the verification and compliance capabilities available through a tracked core channel.

### Fiserv expands Finxact and digital-stack deployment

Queensland Country Bank plans a phased modernization using Fiserv’s cloud-native, API-first Finxact core alongside VeriPark loan-origination, CRM, and digital-banking systems.[REF 1] The broader program also includes Fiserv’s Vision Next card-management solution and is intended to modernize key platforms progressively across the institution’s member touchpoints.[REF 1] The deployment reinforces Fiserv’s channel position among financial institutions ASA may target.

### FIS extends overlap across Forecast and Compass

FIS’s agentic suite is designed to help finance teams manage collections, liquidity, risk, and cash flow from a single view.[REF 3][REF 4] Its forecasting module projects cash across configurable time horizons and uses multiple simulations rather than a single estimate, while the broader suite surfaces risk and recommended actions.[REF 3][REF 4] The signal warrants competitive monitoring across ASA Forecast and Compass but, on the supplied evidence, does not by itself require a quarterly roadmap change.

### ICBA challenges OCC crypto-trust-bank charter authority

ICBA is challenging the OCC’s authority to charter non-depository crypto and stablecoin firms as national trust banks.[REF 5] The litigation could affect regulatory expectations for fintech custody, payments, and account connectivity, with implications for ASA’s platform-risk assumptions and partner strategy.[REF 5]
