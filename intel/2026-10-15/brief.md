## Material signals

### Socure reports strong Q3 bookings and AI-fraud demand

Socure reported $42.7 million in new Q3 bookings, renewed $34.2 million across 75 customers, and entered Q4 tracking toward approximately $450 million in full-year ARR.[REF 1] The company’s reported expansion strengthens competitive pressure in identity verification and AI-driven fraud prevention, particularly for ASA Verify and Auth.

### HKMA establishes responsible-AI framework for banking

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority introduced its “ART” Framework—Accountability, Resilience, and Trust—to guide responsible AI adoption in banking.[REF 2] The framework includes updated supervisory guidance, an AI training-data-sharing platform, further agentic-AI testing through the GenAI Sandbox++, and a planned discussion paper on agentic-AI governance.[REF 2][REF 4] The direction raises expectations for ownership, human oversight, resilience, explainability, privacy, and auditability in AI-enabled financial products.

### Corelation and Unit21 deepen embedded fraud and AML controls

Corelation is integrating Unit21’s fraud and AML platform with KeyStone to support real-time detection and direct account-control actions. The deployment increases competitive pressure around fraud workflow integration across ASA Verify, Auth, and financial-institution platform connectivity.

### OCC identifies debanking policies at nine large banks

The OCC reported that all nine large national banks it reviewed maintained policies or practices that restricted or denied access to some customers, including through broad reputation-risk policies lacking clear, objective, risk-based criteria.[REF 2] The review covered JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citibank, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, Capital One, PNC Bank, TD Bank, and BMO Bank.[REF 2] The CFPB separately announced an initiative to address potential consumer harm identified through the OCC review.[REF 5] The development heightens the importance of auditable decisioning, objective verification criteria, privacy controls, and documented consent for ASA’s financial-data and verification products.

### Alkami customer selects MANTL for digital onboarding

City First Bank selected MANTL’s omnichannel account-opening suite to support customer acquisition and nationwide growth.[REF 5] MANTL’s positioning around automated onboarding, KYC, AML, and BSA workflows strengthens Alkami’s competitive account-opening and identity-verification offering to financial institutions.
