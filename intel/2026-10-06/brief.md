### ICBA challenges OCC crypto trust-charter authority

The Independent Community Bankers of America sued the OCC, arguing that the agency exceeded its authority by approving or conditionally approving national trust-bank charters for crypto firms outside the traditional banking framework.[REF 1] The challenge targets the OCC’s chartering rule, Interpretive Letter 1176, and the conditional charter approval for Protego Holdings Corp.[REF 1] If successful, the case could narrow the regulatory perimeter for fintech banking models and affect ASA’s partner, compliance, and platform-positioning assumptions.[REF 1]

### Fiserv shifts FIUSD strategy

Fiserv changed its original plan to issue FIUSD, while maintaining its commitment to the stablecoin and operating a digital-asset system that hosts the Bank of North Dakota’s Roughrider stablecoin.[REF 2] Fiserv has not specified when it will launch its own stablecoin, and its stated strategy is evolving.[REF 2] The shift could affect ASA Pay interoperability assumptions and Fiserv’s positioning with financial-institution partners.[REF 2]
