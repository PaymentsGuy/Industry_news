## Material signals

### Corelation ecosystem adds Bankjoy deployment

TEN Credit Union selected Bankjoy for digital banking while migrating to Corelation’s KeyStone core. Bankjoy connects to KeyStone through Corelation’s certified KeyBridge integration, reinforcing a competing core-connected distribution path relevant to ASA’s FI channel strategy.[REF 1]

### EPC tightens Verification of Payee matching guidance

The EPC updated its Verification of Payee recommendations with guidance on character handling and beneficiary-name matching before payment initiation. The recommendations address match, close-match, and no-match outcomes and may require ASA Verify and Pay to support standardized name normalization and matching logic.[REF 2]

### Socure extends reusable identity into Stellar

Socure partnered with the Stellar Development Foundation to bring Socure Pass, its reusable identity product, to applications across the Stellar ecosystem. The move expands Socure’s ecosystem-based distribution and is relevant to ASA Auth and Verify positioning.[REF 3]

### FinCEN withdraws proposed digital-asset transaction rules

FinCEN withdrew proposed reporting, verification, and recordkeeping rules covering certain unhosted-wallet and CVC-mixing transactions. The withdrawal changes the digital-asset compliance backdrop for financial-institution connectivity and monitoring but does not, on the supplied evidence, require an immediate ASA roadmap change.[REF 4]

### Alkami emphasizes contextual personalization

Alkami published generational research on using account-holder context to deliver more personalized digital-banking experiences. The signal reinforces demand for contextual insights and next-best recommendations relevant to ASA Compass and personalization across the platform.[REF 5]
