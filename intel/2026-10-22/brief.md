## Material signals

### FinCEN and DOJ preserve AML enforcement posture

FinCEN and DOJ say AML reforms are intended to reduce compliance burdens without weakening enforcement against willful misconduct or high-risk violations [REF 1]. For ASA, the signal reinforces the need for strong financial-crime controls, monitoring, explainability, and partner-governance capabilities across the financial-institution platform.

### OCC finalizes GENIUS Act stablecoin framework

The OCC finalized rules governing permitted payment-stablecoin issuers, certain foreign issuers, and related custody activities for OCC-supervised entities [REF 2]. The framework takes effect January 18, 2027 [REF 2]. It prohibits direct yield payments from stablecoin issuers, while third-party reward or yield arrangements will be evaluated case by case [REF 2]. AML, BSA, and OFAC requirements are excluded from this rule and will be addressed in separate Treasury-coordinated rulemaking [REF 1][REF 2]. The rule changes compliance assumptions for financial institutions and stablecoin partners relevant to ASA platform risk and partner planning.

### FDIC examines agentic AI’s impact on deposits

FDIC Chair Travis Hill said the agency is considering how AI agents could move deposits on behalf of customers and whether existing supervisory guidance is adequate for rapidly evolving generative and agentic AI [REF 3]. The FDIC may solicit public input on the supervisory treatment of these systems [REF 3]. Potential future expectations around agent authority, monitoring, customer consent, and auditability are relevant to ASA’s agentic-finance controls and partner-risk requirements.

### TD and Akoya advance permissioned financial-data connectivity

TD and Akoya plan API-based connections that give customers control over financial-data permissions, including provider selection and revocation [REF 4]. The development advances a secure, credential-free aggregation model relevant to ASA Vault connectivity, privacy positioning, and financial-institution distribution.

### Alkami integrates automated lending calculations and compliance validation

Alkami announced a MANTL integration with Carleton that automates loan calculations, compliance validation, and disclosures within loan origination workflows [REF 5]. The capability strengthens Alkami’s lending-platform proposition, although the announcement does not directly affect ASA’s listed product lines.
