### Regulators reinforce AML control expectations through Amex enforcement

### Mastercard to enable offline payments across Europe

Mastercard will require newly issued European cards to support offline payments beginning in February 2027, with new and replacement payment terminals required to support the capability by May 2027.[REF 3] Offline authorization will allow cardholders to pay during power or network outages, subject to limits set by the issuing bank, with transactions submitted for clearing after connectivity is restored.[REF 3] The initiative is relevant to ASA Pay’s reliability and offline-authorization roadmap, but the supplied evidence does not indicate an immediate ASA decision requirement.[REF 3]
