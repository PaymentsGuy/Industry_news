**This week's headline:** FDX–Yodlee–Alkami moves make FDX APIs the de facto baseline for bank data connectivity as FinCEN proposes a new AML/CFT regime.

## TL;DR

1. Alkami is moving its aggregation to **FDX APIs with Yodlee**, signaling that FDX-native, API-only connectivity is becoming table stakes for bank digital channels ASA competes against.[REF 1]  
2. FinCEN and the banking agencies proposed **modernized AML/CFT program rules**, while a parallel executive-order/FATF-aligned package outlines broader expectations for banks and MSBs, raising the compliance bar for ASA’s FI customers.[REF 2][REF 3]  
3. Multiple core and digital-banking partners (Corelation, Q2, Nymbus, Fiserv) launched or deepened **agentic AI and MCP-style capabilities**, accelerating expectations that core and channel platforms natively expose AI agents and AI dev environments.[REF 4][REF 5][REF 6][REF 7]

## By roadmap area

**Vault.** Alkami announced a new integration to **transition its data aggregation to the FDX API standard with Yodlee**, emphasizing improved performance, lower friction, and stronger user control for open-banking connectivity.[REF 1] This cements FDX APIs as a default expectation inside a major digital-banking channel ASA will frequently encounter in mid-market FI sales. Recommendation: **Investigate** near-term FDX compatibility and Alkami integration positioning to avoid being screened out on standards by Q4 2026.

**Compass.** Personetics launched an **MCP server** that lets banks develop agentic AI apps on top of its financial-intelligence data, and Flybits launched an **agentic banking capability** to orchestrate AI-guided customer journeys.[REF 4][REF 8] This is direct-adjacent validation that “agentic PFM/insights” is moving from slideware into deployable infrastructure that FI buyers can touch. Recommendation: **Monitor** competitive agentic launches closely and align Compass demo narrative to “agentic copilots” by Q3 roadmap review.

**Auth.** Alloy pushed **AI-driven perpetual KYC**, and Socure continued to expand its identity/fraud stack via a Checkr Trust partnership and a Thomson Reuters tie-up, while FinCEN/EO commentary points to tighter KYC/CIP expectations.[REF 3][REF 9][REF 10][REF 11] These moves tilt the market toward continuous identity-risk monitoring supplied by specialist vendors. Recommendation: **Investigate** whether ASA Auth should tightly align to one or two of these partners vs. building more native risk signals, ahead of 2027 planning.

**Verify.** FinCEN’s proposed AML/CFT program update plus the broader EO/FinCEN analysis will push banks and MSBs toward more robust, ongoing identity and income verification workflows.[REF 2][REF 3] Alloy’s perpetual-KYC product and Socure’s government-focused unified identity/fraud solution set a benchmark for “always-on” verification capabilities adjacent to ASA Verify.[REF 9][REF 10] Recommendation: **Monitor** regulatory rule text as it hardens and ensure Verify messaging explicitly supports perpetual KYC/AML use cases by early 2027.

**Pay.** No new direct-payments launches this week, but FDIC/FinCEN/FDIC-capital commentary continues to flag **heightened scrutiny of bank–fintech partnership and capital/risk treatment**, which will affect pay-by-bank and deposit-movement partners ASA may integrate with.[REF 5][REF 12] Recommendation: **Monitor** for final capital/partnership guidance that might constrain which sponsor banks ASA can rely on for Pay flows.

**One View.** No new enrichment or PFM launches beyond already-tracked Bud and Plaid items, but Personetics and Flybits’ agentic offerings show that **insights and personalization layers are being wired directly into FI digital channels**, not just third-party apps.[REF 4][REF 8] Recommendation: **Monitor** FI adoption stories of agentic insight products to calibrate One View’s “embedded in the bank app” pitch.

**Forecast.** Personetics and Atomic’s deposit-growth and measurement partnerships (prior ledger) are now complemented by broader EO/FinCEN shifts that will make cash-flow-aware risk and affordability assessment more important for lenders.[REF 3][REF 13] Recommendation: **Monitor** for any explicit regulatory references to cash-flow underwriting; no immediate **Decision needed**.

**Horizontal.** FinCEN and the banking agencies proposed rule changes to **update AML/CFT programs**, and multiple analyses of an executive order and FinCEN proposals highlight a larger shift in federal expectations for AML, customer identification, and underwriting.[REF 2][REF 3][REF 14] Corelation and Cotribute launched AI agents for credit unions, Q2 rolled out an AI assistant and Q2 Code dev environment, Nymbus launched a secure MCP server, and Fiserv is co-building AI agents with six banks and OpenAI, together signaling that ASA’s core and digital partners are racing to be the primary AI surface.[REF 4][REF 5][REF 6][REF 7] Recommendation: **Decision needed by 2026-09-30** on whether ASA wants to appear inside these partner-native AI assistants as “the intelligence spine,” or continue to push ASA-branded surfaces.
