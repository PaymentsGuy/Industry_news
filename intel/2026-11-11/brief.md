### BPI urges broader AML obligations for digital-asset intermediaries

BPI called on FinCEN and Treasury to designate digital-asset service providers—including entities that custody, transfer, exchange, or facilitate customer transactions—as financial institutions under the Bank Secrecy Act. It also urged applying obligations to persons operating or controlling decentralized-finance arrangements and improving information sharing across the digital-asset payment chain.[REF 1]

This is an industry recommendation rather than an adopted requirement. If pursued, it could expand AML, sanctions, reporting, recordkeeping, customer-information, and data-sharing expectations across financial institutions and digital-asset channels connected to ASA’s platform.[REF 1]
