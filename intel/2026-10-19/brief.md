## Material signals

### FCA calls for disclosures on AI financial advice

The UK FCA called on AI providers to use clear, proportionate, and consistent disclaimers explaining the limitations of AI-generated financial information and advice, including advice delivered through agentic AI.[REF 1] The signal is relevant to ASA’s AI-enabled financial guidance because disclosures, point-of-delivery warnings, and accountability guardrails may become expected controls.

### Hong Kong banks test AI deepfake detection

Banks participating in the HKMA’s GenAI Sandbox reported testing AI-powered deepfake detection for identity and fraud controls.[REF 2] Ping An Digital Bank reported more than 96% detection accuracy in testing, while HSBC reported that none of approximately 20 test deepfakes bypassed its controls.[REF 2] The activity supports continued attention to AI-enhanced identity verification and fraud prevention for ASA Verify and Auth.

### Jack Henry and Google Cloud advance tokenized deposits

Jack Henry expanded its Google Cloud relationship to integrate with Google Cloud Universal Ledger, enabling banks and credit unions to offer tokenized deposits.[REF 3][REF 4] The planned infrastructure supports tokenization and transfer of money and assets on a shared real-time record, with programmable, 24/7 settlement capabilities.[REF 3] This could affect ASA Pay interoperability and platform positioning as core providers extend into tokenized payment and settlement services.

### Jack Henry integrates Google Cloud ledger for programmable settlement

Jack Henry’s announced integration with Google Cloud Universal Ledger is intended to support tokenized deposits, programmable payments, and near-real-time settlement for banks and credit unions.[REF 4] The integration also describes automated clearing and multilateral netting, potentially reducing reliance on prefunded accounts and improving liquidity efficiency.[REF 4] The development warrants monitoring for overlap with ASA Pay and the evolving settlement layer offered through bank-core distribution.

### Socure reports accelerated identity-market growth

Socure reported accelerated Q3 growth and more than 3,000 customer additions amid increased demand for identity verification and fraud prevention.[REF 5] The reported expansion strengthens competitive pressure around ASA Verify and Auth, while the broader rise of AI-enabled fraud increases demand for integrated identity and transaction-risk controls.
