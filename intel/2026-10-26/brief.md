## Material signals

### FCA to test agentic open-finance infrastructure

The FCA will test open-finance infrastructure models for agentic services, including consent, verification, delegation, monitoring, and accountability. This could shape future expectations for ASA authentication, verification, consent management, and platform-trust architecture.[REF 1]

### TD selects Akoya for secure API-based open banking

TD Bank selected Akoya to provide secure API-based access for financial applications and data aggregators, with phased onboarding beginning in Q4 2026. The development reinforces API-first aggregation and has direct relevance to ASA Vault connectivity, One View data access, and privacy positioning.[REF 2]

### Corelation credit union selects Bankjoy integration

TEN Credit Union selected Bankjoy for digital banking alongside its conversion to Corelation’s KeyStone core. The certified integration strengthens a competing digital-banking path within the Corelation ecosystem and increases the strategic value of direct core connectivity for ASA distribution.[REF 3]

### HKMA establishes ART framework for agentic banking AI

HKMA introduced the ART framework, centered on accountability, resilience, and trust, with planned testing of agentic AI applications and future governance work addressing intent-anchored accountability, material-agent registries, and intervention mechanisms.[REF 4] The framework supports Vault-level requirements for agent governance, data lineage, intervention controls, and privacy safeguards.[REF 4]

### Sezzle applies for OCC national bank charter

Sezzle applied for an OCC national bank charter to expand into deposits, checking, savings, and credit products. The application signals continued fintech-to-bank expansion under federal oversight and could increase demand for financial-data and intelligence capabilities relevant to ASA.[REF 5]

### Jack Henry plans tokenized-deposit capability

Jack Henry plans to integrate Google Cloud’s Universal Ledger to support tokenized deposits and programmable settlement for financial institutions. The move extends Jack Henry’s core-channel position into emerging payment infrastructure and could create future implications for ASA Pay and partner strategy.[REF 6]

### Sezzle pivots from planned ILC to national-bank application

A separate report describes Sezzle’s national-bank filing as a shift away from its planned Utah industrial-loan-company structure. The change reinforces the broader trend of fintechs seeking direct regulated-bank status and is relevant to ASA’s financial-institution connectivity and regulatory positioning.[REF 7]
