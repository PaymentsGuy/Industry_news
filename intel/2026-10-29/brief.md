## Material signals

### Socure appoints Rohan Sivaram as CFO

Socure appointed Rohan Sivaram as chief financial officer, with responsibility for finance, operations, capital markets, and pricing economics. The leadership change could influence commercial scaling and pricing strategy around ASA-relevant identity and risk capabilities in Verify and Auth.[REF 1]

### Jack Henry expands stablecoin distribution through Stablecore

Stablecore joined Jack Henry’s integration network, enabling approximately 1,600 U.S. banks and credit unions to offer stablecoin and tokenized-asset services. The move broadens Jack Henry’s embedded payments and digital-asset proposition across a key ASA distribution channel, with implications for Pay and platform positioning.[REF 2]

### Q2 Holdings appoints CFO Jonathan Price as co-CEO

Q2 Holdings appointed CFO Jonathan Price as co-CEO alongside Matt Flake, effective October 29, while retaining Price as CFO during a search for his successor; Price also joins the board.[REF 3] The change warrants attention for potential shifts in Q2’s financial-institution platform strategy, AI roadmap, and partnership priorities.

### U.S. banks block external AI-agent access

Major U.S. banks are restricting external AI agents from accessing customer accounts because of security, fraud, credential, and liability concerns. Reported controls include blocking agents from accessing accounts or moving money, while banks acknowledge that self-identifying agents may not be the only agents reaching their systems.[REF 4] The market direction supports controlled or read-only agent access and strengthens the relevance of ASA Auth, Verify, and Vault capabilities for consent, identity, permissions, and auditability.
