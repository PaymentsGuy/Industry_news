## Material signals

### Mahalo wins 5Point Credit Union digital-banking mandate

5Point Credit Union selected Mahalo Banking for digital self-service and expanded business-banking capabilities, with connectivity to Corelation KeyStone. The win extends a competing digital-banking ecosystem into another Corelation-connected institution and is relevant to ASA One View and channel strategy.[REF 1]

### FinCEN proposes revised customer-due-diligence requirements

FinCEN proposed changes that would let covered institutions obtain additional beneficial-owner information for risk mitigation, remove the requirement to identify and verify beneficial owners each time an existing legal-entity customer opens another account, and specify information institutions must obtain about beneficial owners.[REF 2] The proposal could increase demand for auditable identity-verification, risk-based review, and re-verification workflows relevant to ASA Verify and ASA Auth.[REF 2]

### Dave applies for a full-service national-bank charter

Dave filed applications with the OCC and FDIC to establish Dave Bank, N.A., a proposed insured national bank that would offer consumer deposit and credit products directly under federal supervision if approved.[REF 3] The move could change partner-bank economics and increase competitive pressure in financial infrastructure and embedded banking; approvals remain pending.[REF 3]

### Jack Henry reports 17 competitive core wins

Jack Henry reported 17 competitive core-banking wins, describing the result as its strongest third quarter for new core wins in seven years.[REF 4] The reported momentum could expand Jack Henry’s distribution reach and intensify competition for ASA’s financial-institution channel strategy.
