## Material signals

### Avant applies for national bank charter

Avant filed applications with the OCC and FDIC on September 18, 2026, to establish Avant Bank, N.A., an insured national bank. The proposed bank would support an integrated platform combining credit, banking products, and financial-health tools.[REF 1]

The move would increase competition for integrated financial experiences and heightens the importance of flexible financial-institution connectivity for ASA’s horizontal roadmap.[REF 1]

### Agora receives conditional approval for national trust bank

The OCC granted Agora preliminary conditional approval to establish a national trust bank. The proposed institution would bring Agora’s stablecoin, custody, and transaction infrastructure under federal supervision.[REF 2]

The development is relevant to ASA Pay because federally supervised stablecoin and payment infrastructure could influence partnership models and the regulated digital-asset ecosystem.[REF 2]

### Plaid expands into AI-driven credit, fraud, and payment risk

Plaid launched AI models intended to improve lending decisions, fraud detection, and payment-risk management for financial firms. The launch includes expanded credit-risk models, a fraud foundation model, and sequential transaction analysis for payment risk.[REF 3]

The expansion broadens Plaid’s competitive position across workflows adjacent to ASA Verify and ASA Pay, beyond its established connectivity and data-intelligence footprint.[REF 3]

### Lumin Digital launches behavioral-intelligence capability

Lumin Digital launched Lumin Signal for banks and credit unions, providing behavioral analytics, in-app guidance, and surveys within its digital-banking platform. The capability is designed to identify user friction, improve feature adoption, and measure digital-experience ROI.[REF 4]

Lumin’s move extends its platform from transaction and channel visibility into actionable digital-experience intelligence, increasing competitive pressure around ASA Compass and One View insights.[REF 4]
